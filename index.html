On the label

What's actually in the bag

Pet food labels are legally allowed to be vague. Ours aren't. Every food we carry names its protein by animal, lists its guaranteed analysis, and carries an AAFCO statement for a specific life stage — not a hedge like "for all life stages" stretched over a puppy and a fourteen-year-old terrier.

If a bag doesn't tell you its calories per cup, we don't stock it. You can't portion what you can't measure.
